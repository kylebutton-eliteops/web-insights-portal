.csv, .csv.gz, .zip or .xlsx · allowed traffic, last 14 days
Any CSV works: a ZIA export, a SIEM or ADX pull, or your own list. It needs a URL, host or domain column; a header row is optional. If there's no header, column 1 is read as the URL and a numeric column 2 as the hit count. Columns named hits or count are used as hit counts.
